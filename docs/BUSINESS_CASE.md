# Business case — Prada-inspired Customer Feedback Intelligence

> A Prada customer experience analyst receives feedback from post-purchase surveys, customer service interactions and product reviews. The application uses AI to classify feedback, identify recurring issues, and produce an evidence-based summary with recommended actions.

**Context:** Fictional scenario inspired by a luxury brand's customer-experience operations. Not an Adobe or Prada product, endorsement, requirement, or integration. All included example records are synthetic.

**Persona:** A non-technical customer-experience analyst tasked with discovering common issues across markets, product categories, and channels.

**Problem:** Different feedback sources create fragmented data. An analyst must read comments individually, decide how to categorize them, and turn anecdotal feedback into actionable priorities.

**Value proposition:** Reduce manual categorization effort, provide traceable issue rankings and summaries, and surface useful investigative next steps while preserving human decision-making.

**Success criteria for the MVP:** Upload a CSV with no preloaded data; classify each submitted record; provide counts and evidence IDs; show segment-specific insights; allow analysts to inspect the original feedback; and degrade gracefully if executive summary generation fails.
